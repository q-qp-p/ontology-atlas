## 2026-10-15 — A flat-map click selects; zoom is the reader's gesture

**Why**: at 1920 a click on the meaning-layer domain zoomed the camera 0.890 → 1.601, folded the toolbar (1484 → 1092 px, pill moved 353 px, utility lane and fit tile gone) and INDEX; one Escape left the selection on and the camera 140 px off.
**Prior**: narrows 2026-10-02 "Flat ... shows detail by zoom" (`decisions/2026-10-02-flat-dial-88b537fc-3831-4e9d-823f-0465c90a65a8.md`): detail still arrives by zoom, but never as a click's side effect. Applies the owner rule since 2026-07 that a click never re-lays out the map without an explicit affordance. Overturns the flat map's two-press Escape (popover, then deselect); the 3D dome keeps it.
**Decision**: on Flat a click selects and opens the panel at the same scale; the camera slides sideways on `SPRING.canvas` only when the panel would cover the node, and a deselect undoes exactly that slide. Double-click, Enter and the wheel zoom. Only chrome under the panel fades in place (`--motion-base`, exit ease); INDEX stays as left. One Escape clears the selection and returns camera and chrome. The canvas frame shows only when focus arrives by Tab.
**Dissent**: a click no longer frames a far node's ego graph; a reader who wanted it must double-click. Search and INDEX picks still frame.
**Falsifier**: the 2026-10-02 falsifier stands; also reopen on a click that moves the camera scale, or an Escape that lands more than 1 px from the pre-click camera.
